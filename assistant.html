Why it matters

Why Correct Disposal Matters

Putting an item in the right bin decides whether it is recovered, treated safely or ends up polluting the environment.

♻️

Recovers resources

Clean, separated paper, metal, glass and plastic can be recycled into new products instead of being dumped.

🌱

Turns food into compost

Wet waste kept separate can be composted, returning nutrients to the soil instead of rotting in landfills.

💧

Prevents pollution

Batteries, chemicals and electronics can leak harmful substances into soil and water if thrown in household bins.

🧤

Protects waste workers

Wrapped sanitary waste, labelled sharp objects and separated chemicals keep the people who handle our waste safe.

Practical tips

5 Waste-Disposal Tips for Every Home

  1. 1

    When in doubt, keep it separate

    If you are unsure about an item, keep it aside instead of mixing it with other waste, and ask your local collector.

  2. 2

    Empty, rinse and dry recyclables

    Food and liquids make paper, plastic and cans hard to recycle. A quick rinse keeps them clean and odour-free.

  3. 3

    Never burn waste

    Burning plastic, leaves or wires releases harmful smoke. Hand waste over to collection instead.

  4. 4

    Use authorised channels for special waste

    Give e-waste, batteries and household chemicals to authorised collection centres or take-back programmes.

  5. 5

    Wrap and label sanitary and sharp waste

    Wrap pads, diapers, blades and broken glass in paper and mark them, so they can be handled safely.

ℹ️

Please note

The advice on this page is general guidance for Indian households. Waste collection methods, segregation categories and bin colours can vary from one municipality or locality to another. Always follow the official instructions of your local municipal body (Nagar Nigam / Municipal Corporation) or housing society.
