Calculate Your Household Waste

Understand how much waste your household generates every day.

Enter your daily waste

Enter roughly how many kilograms per day your household throws away in each category. Leave a field empty if it is zero.

💡 Tip: 1 kg = 1000 g. A small bowl of vegetable peels is roughly 0.2–0.3 kg. You can use decimals like 0.5 or 1.25.

How we calculate

  • Daily totalsum of all 6 categories
  • Weeklydaily total × 7
  • Monthlydaily total × 30
  • Category %(category ÷ daily total) × 100

These are simple estimates based on the numbers you enter. Actual waste changes from day to day.
