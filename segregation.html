Quick Tips

5 Habits for Easy Segregation at Home

  1. 01

    Keep separate bins in the kitchen

    Place at least two bins — one for wet and one for dry waste — where waste is actually created, so sorting takes no extra effort.

  2. 02

    Rinse and dry before you throw

    A quick rinse of milk pouches, cans and containers keeps recyclables clean and stops bad smells and insects.

  3. 03

    Keep a box for e-waste and hazardous items

    Collect batteries, old chargers, bulbs and expired medicines in a separate box and drop them at collection points together.

  4. 04

    Wrap sanitary waste and sharp objects

    Wrap pads, diapers, blades and broken glass in paper and mark them, so waste workers aren't hurt or exposed to infection.

  5. 05

    Reduce and reuse first

    Carry a cloth bag and a water bottle, refuse single-use plastic and repair before replacing. The best waste is the waste you never create.

Important note

Waste collection methods, bin colour-coding and category rules can vary between cities and even between localities. This guide gives general advice for Indian households. Always follow the guidelines issued by your local municipal body (Nagar Nigam / Municipal Corporation) or housing society.
